# Portfolio CV Source Map

## Purpose

This document records how the supplied CV set was normalized into the public portfolio. It is an internal editorial reference, not a public portfolio page.

## Core positioning

Public title:

> Product Designer · Business Analyst · Solution Consultant

Supporting evidence appears consistently across the primary UI/UX, Digital Solution Consultant, and Business Analyst CVs. AI is presented as an enabled workflow/capability, not as a claim of machine-learning engineering.

Since 2026-11-27 the site frames this positioning around the delivery workflow: the four capability groups follow the order requirements → design → solution → delivery, and the copy presents AI as something applied at each stage. Every AI claim on the site maps to a line in the CVs (AI-assisted research, prototyping, documentation, design iteration, workflow automation, prompt engineering, AI-assisted development, agentic AI concepts). The site does not claim AI use in testing, any productivity figure, or authorship of any company framework.

## Normalization decisions

- Experience is stated by discipline rather than added across overlapping employment and freelance periods.
- Role names are consolidated where CV versions use different emphases for the same period.
- BeeTrade is dated `2024 — Present` because CV versions disagree on the exact start month.
- Freelance/independent consulting is stated as `2022 — Present`; individual engagements may have ended earlier.
- The career timeline follows the unified timeline in [CV.md](CV.md): current roles first (the full-time role, then the parallel consulting work), then earlier roles in reverse order. Company names within a period are listed in chronological order.
- Phone numbers are not published on the web portfolio. Email is the only public contact channel.
- No quantitative business or usability metric is published because the CV set does not provide a verifiable source for one.
- Client names from local SME engagements are generalized in public project content.

## Future updates

When adding a project to `assets/js/portfolio-data.js`, record:

1. Source document or approved project brief.
2. Exact role and timeframe.
3. Public-safe challenge statement.
4. Verified contributions.
5. Deliverable types that may be named.
6. Qualitative or quantitative outcome with its evidence source.
7. Information intentionally omitted.
